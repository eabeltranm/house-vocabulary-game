💡 Quick Guide: Present vs. Past

1. Verbs (Verb *be*)

  • Present: There is / There's / He is
  • Past: There was / We were / He was

2. Time Words

  • Present: every week / usually / every month / today
  • Past: yesterday / last week / last month / last year
